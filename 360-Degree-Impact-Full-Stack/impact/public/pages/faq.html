Frequently asked questions.

Which communication channels can I enquire about?

360° Impact offers SMS, WhatsApp, email, voice and RCS, along with integrations and automation. See all options on the Services page.

Can I ask about more than one service?

Yes. Select Multiple channels on the demo request form and describe what you need.

Can you help with SMS registration and templates?

You can enquire about DLT registration and template support for your SMS campaigns.

What happens after I request a demo?

Your request goes to the 360° Impact team for follow-up using the work email you provide.
